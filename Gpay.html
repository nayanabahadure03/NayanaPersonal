Proposed MVP 2:00–2:25

Introducing Google Pay Money Check-In.

A lightweight weekly habit that helps users review recent Google Pay spending and reconnect it with the savings intention they set at the start of the month.

1

Set intention

User enters the amount they hope to save or invest this month.

2

Review spending

User sees a simple weekly summary of recent Google Pay spending.

3

Reflect

User marks whether they feel On Track, Not Sure or Behind.

4

Build a habit

A weekly Money Awareness Streak rewards review consistency, not lower spending.

The MVP does one focused job: move money awareness earlier in the month.

Step 1

Make the intention visible

The user records what they already intend to protect. No full budget setup is required.

Step 2

Bring awareness forward

A weekly review surfaces recent Google Pay spending before the user reaches the end of the month.

Step 3

Avoid false precision

The user decides whether they feel On Track, Not Sure or Behind rather than Google Pay claiming it knows their complete financial position.

Step 4

Reward awareness

The streak reinforces the act of reviewing. It does not reward spending less.

MVP boundary: Money Check-In does not prevent medical, family or other unexpected expenses; automatically protect savings; calculate a perfect safe-to-spend amount; or provide financial advice.
